# Orbit Analytics — data policy

## Retention

Raw event data is retained for twelve months on the Growth plan and twenty four
months on Enterprise. Aggregated metrics are kept indefinitely and are
unaffected by raw data expiry.

## Deletion requests

A deletion request for an individual removes their events and profile within
thirty days across primary storage and backups. A confirmation report is
available through the dashboard once processing completes.

## Residency

Workspaces can be provisioned in the European Union or the United States region.
The region is fixed at creation time and cannot be changed without a full
migration, which support performs on request.

## Exports

Any table or chart can be exported to CSV. Exports above one hundred thousand
rows are prepared asynchronously and emailed as a download link that expires
after seven days.
