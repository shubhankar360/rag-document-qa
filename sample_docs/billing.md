# Orbit Analytics — billing

## Cycle and invoices

Billing runs on a monthly cycle by default. The invoice is generated on the same
calendar day the workspace first subscribed, and charges are collected
automatically from the card on file.

Switching from monthly to annual billing applies a twenty percent discount. The
change takes effect immediately and any unused portion of the current month is
credited to the account.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, we retry three times over seven days. The workspace
keeps full access during the retry window and an email is sent to every
workspace administrator.

## Cancellation and refunds

A subscription can be cancelled at any time. The workspace stays on the paid
plan until the end of the period already paid for, then reverts to the free
tier. Downgrading keeps all historical data but limits querying to the most
recent ninety days.

Refunds are issued for the current billing period only and must be requested
within fourteen days of the charge.
